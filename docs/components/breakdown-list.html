Breakdown List

Rows of a name, its count and its share, each with a thin bar for the share: traffic source, country, device. Added 23 Nov 2026, React only. Implemented in react/src/components/BreakdownList.tsx; the bars are the Meter track.

Share scale (default)

A bar is as long as the share: 50% fills half of the track.

  • Canada (CA)150%
  • Egypt (EG)150%

Relative scale

The row with the largest share fills the track and the others are scaled to it. Use it when every share is small and the bars would all look empty.

  • Singapore13,16874.7%
  • China1,79310.2%
  • United States1,1206.4%

Empty

No device data in this period.

Accessibility

The row text (name, count, share) is the information. The bar repeats it, so it is aria-hidden and has no meter role (a list of 20 meters would only add noise). The share text uses --vs-color-text-body (14:1 on white); the muted grey is only 2.8:1. A row whose share is not a number shows its count and no bar or percent. Names are cut with an ellipsis, never wrapped.

Not included: a layout with the bar beside the name (the phone row), stacked multi-colour bars and circular charts.
