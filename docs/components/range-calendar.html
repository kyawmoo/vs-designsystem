Range Calendar

Pick a From and a To day: click one day, then another. One or two months side by side. Implemented in react/src/components/RangeCalendar.tsx (React only; no Blade version). It shows the calendar only: Apply / Cancel and the From / To fields belong to whatever opens it (a Popover).

03 // Behaviour

Behaviour

What the component does for you.

SituationWhat happens
First clickonChange({ from, to: null }); moving over other days shades a preview.
Second clickonChange({ from, to }), always in order: picked in reverse they swap by themselves. The same day twice is a one-day range.
Click after a finished rangeStarts a new range.
Days outside minDate..maxDateDisabled: not clickable and skipped by the keyboard. maxDate defaults to today.
maxDays (default 365)While the second date is pending, days that would make a longer range are disabled, so a too-long range cannot be picked.
Month arrowsThe next arrow is off on the last month that can be picked, the previous arrow on the first.
KeyboardOne tab stop; arrows move a day / a week; Home / End the week's start / end; PageUp / PageDown a month; Enter or Space picks. The month scrolls into view.
NamesEach day button is named by its full date ("14 October 2026"); aria-pressed on the picked ends, aria-current="date" on today.
DatesCalendar days as 'YYYY-MM-DD' text (no time, no time zone). Helpers exported: formatDMY (14 Oct 2026), formatRangeLabel (14–20 Oct), daysInclusive, addDays, isIsoDate, todayIsoDate.
