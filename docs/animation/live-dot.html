Live Dot

A small dot with a soft ripple that grows and fades on repeat. It marks something that is live, online or the newest: a status next to a label, a dot on an avatar, the end of a chart line. React LiveDot, Blade <x-vs-live-dot>; LineChart and BarChart draw the same dot on the newest reading of every line.

Sizes

sm, md (default), lg
sm · 5px md · 8px lg · 12px

Colours

Any design token (var(--vs-…)) or a hex colour. Default: the brand green --vs-color-brand-primary.

  • Motion. The ripple grows from the dot to 3.5x (sm), 5.5x (md) or 4x (lg) while it fades from 50% to 0, every 2.4s, eased with --vs-motion-ease-decelerate. With reduced motion the dot stays and the ripple does not run.
  • Ring. The dot has a 2px ring in --vs-color-surface (1.5px on sm), so it stays visible on a line or an avatar.
  • Meaning. Colour is not the only signal: say what the dot means in the text next to it, or give it a label.
  • In charts. LineChart and BarChart (liveDot, default on) place the same dot over the canvas on the newest reading up to today, fading in once the line has drawn up to it; a sparkline gets sm; BarChart lineStyle="markers" keeps its large today dot and only the ripple is added.
  • One CSS block. The rules are the vs-live-dot block, identical in react/src/styles.css and blade/components.css; check:tokens fails if they drift apart, if the block reads a token that does not exist, or if reduced motion no longer stops the ripple.
